# Alethea

> Alethea is an AI-powered narrative and disinformation risk intelligence company.
> Its platform detects coordinated inauthentic behavior, influence operations and
> emerging online narratives before they escalate into reputational, physical or
> operational crises. Alethea sells to enterprises through a demo-and-contract
> motion; there is no self-service signup, no published pricing and no public
> developer program.

## API status (read this before looking for a spec)

- No public developer portal, API reference, OpenAPI/AsyncAPI/GraphQL spec, SDK,
  CLI, Postman collection or sandbox is published anywhere on alethea.com.
- Alethea's own Artemis product page states that "Teams can also integrate Artemis
  data directly into their existing workflows through a robust external API." No
  documentation for that API is published publicly; access is arranged through the
  sales motion.
- The Artemis customer application runs at https://artemis.alethea.com. Its backend
  is mounted at /api/v2 and /api/v3 and answers HTTP 401 to every unauthenticated
  request. It is the application's own tenant-scoped backend, not a documented
  developer API, and API Evangelist has published no contract derived from it.
- docs., api., developer. and developers.alethea.com do not resolve in DNS.

## Products

- [Artemis](https://alethea.com/platform/artemis): threat-intelligence and
  narrative-risk platform — early detection of false or threatening narratives,
  attribution of who is behind them, and mitigation context. Pairs Reality
  Defender's synthetic-media detection with Alethea's threat intelligence.
- [Risk Radar](https://alethea.com/platform/risk-radar): narrative and threat
  visibility dashboard.
- [Takedowns](https://alethea.com/platform/takedowns): agentic AI takedown workflow
  for automated mitigation of malicious content.
- [Alethea Insights](https://alethea.com/platform/alethea-insights): curated macro
  threat analysis and intelligence reporting.
